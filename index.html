Before you enquire

A few helpful answers

Who are appointments for?

We welcome female clients, including girls. Parents are welcome to ask us about a suitable style when enquiring.

Does an enquiry confirm my appointment?

No. Appointments are arranged individually. Wait for us to confirm availability and the booking details.

How much will my braids cost?

Prices are by individual quote. Share your preferred style, size and length so we can confirm the price before you book.

Can you supply extensions?

Yes, we can supply braiding hair. Ask about the options and agree on what is needed when enquiring.

How should I prepare my hair?

Please have your hair washed, detangled and blow-dried before your appointment.
