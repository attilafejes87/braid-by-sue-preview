Before you enquire

A few helpful answers

Who are appointments for?

We welcome adults and children. Parents are welcome to ask us about a suitable style when enquiring.

Does an enquiry confirm my appointment?

No. Appointments are arranged individually. Wait for us to confirm availability and the booking details.

How much will my braids cost?

Our booking calendar shows prices for each style, size and length. For a custom style or extensions, ask us to confirm the total price before booking.

Can you supply extensions?

Yes, we can supply braiding hair. Ask about the options and agree on what is needed when enquiring.

How should I prepare my hair?

Please have your hair washed, detangled and blow-dried before your appointment.

How long should I keep my braids in?

The right wear time depends on your style, hair and scalp. Plan to remove braids within six weeks, or sooner if they pull or cause discomfort. Read our guide to braid wear time.

Can I wash my hair while it is braided?

Yes. Keep your scalp and natural hair clean with gentle washing, thorough rinsing and careful drying. The right routine depends on your scalp and the products you use. See our washing and drying tips.

How should I protect my braids at night?

Gather them loosely and use a comfortable satin or silk bonnet, scarf or pillowcase. Avoid pulling your hairline with a tight tie or covering. Read our bedtime care tips.
